# Case Study 1: Kohl's – Smartphone Targeting

### Problem

How can a retailer send the right offer at exactly the right moment?

### Data Used

Kohl's uses:

- Smartphone Wi-Fi connections
- Customer location inside the store
- Online browsing behavior
- Customer purchase history

When a customer enters the store and connects to Wi-Fi, Kohl's can determine their location inside the store. :contentReference[oaicite:2]{index=2}

---

### Example

Suppose a customer is standing in front of the shoe section.

Kohl's can immediately send:

- A shoe discount coupon
- A promotional text message
- A personalized offer

directly to that customer. :contentReference[oaicite:3]{index=3}

---

### Analytics Insight

Marketing success depends on:

> Right product + Right customer + Right place + Right time

A shoe coupon is most valuable when the customer is already standing in the shoe aisle. :contentReference[oaicite:4]{index=4}

### Reality

- Time period
- Location

- Story characteristics

# Major Lessons from All Case Studies

## 1. Better Data Creates Better Decisions

Winning companies collect:

- Location data
- Transaction data
- Social data
- Behavioral data
- Sensor data

instead of relying only on traditional reports. :contentReference[oaicite:27]{index=27}

---

## 2. Analytics Must Lead to Action

Analytics is not about reports.

Analytics should answer:

> What action should we take next?

:contentReference[oaicite:28]{index=28}

---

## 3. Predictive Analytics Is More Valuable Than Descriptive Analytics

Descriptive:

"What happened?"

Predictive:

"What will happen?"

Prescriptive:

"What should we do about it?" :contentReference[oaicite:29]{index=29}

---

## 4. Customer-Level Analytics Is the Future

Instead of analyzing stores or regions, companies increasingly analyze:

- Individual customers
- Individual behaviors
- Individual preferences

to personalize decisions. :contentReference[oaicite:30]{index=30}
